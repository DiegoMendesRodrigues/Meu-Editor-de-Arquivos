## Product name

Meu Editor de Arquivos

## Description

Meu Editor de Arquivos is a lightweight, free and open-source text and code editor, built for developers, system administrators, IT professionals and anyone who works with text files, scripts, logs and configuration files.

Markdown documents open already formatted and stay editable: apply headings, lists, bold, italic, links and tables from the toolbar and, when you need to, switch to plain text with one click. JSON files appear as a collapsible tree, with the exact location of syntax errors.

For source code, the editor offers syntax highlighting and code folding in more than 30 languages, multiple cursors, column selection, find and replace with regular expressions in the document or in entire folders, macros and snippets.

The spell checker works in six languages, with built-in dictionaries and no internet connection. The interface is also available in six languages: Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese, English, Spanish, French and German.

With a free account, your files live in cloud folders and your open tabs, including text you have not saved yet, show up on your other computers. Without an account, everything works offline and your files stay only on your computer.

When you close the app, nothing is lost: your documents come back just as you left them the next time you open it.
